## Introduction

In 2022, a website, a twitter, and youtube channel, all connected by an ARG were created. They were made for one person. How do I know that? Because I made it. For my fiancée. Here's the backstory.

## Step 0: get into ARGs

I got my start creating such stuff way back in late 2016. I wasn't all that great at anything back then, but I was inspired by the [Sombra ARG](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Sombra_ARG) and the stuff [Game Detectives](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/) worked on. As you could imagine, most of my work at the time was puzzle-focused.

Eventually I stumbled across [Night Mind](https://www.youtube.com/@NightMind) and that kickstarted my narrative muscles, and that, too, was very predictable. I made a web series called Monster Hunters based on hunting the Slenderverse monsters. Eventually my skills and creativity were able to mix my two favorite aspects of ARGS, and I made some more original works. When I started Kin, I already had some other works under my belt. 

## Step 1: Failed SCP Clone

First, I was getting very into SCP and tried to make my own shared writing project. Fillmore Feelgoode's Party Emporium! It was a forum that acted as a website for the Emporium. Kane Parsons', (the Backrooms guy), partygoers shared a name with one of the artificial factions of the Emporium. Entirely unrelated, obviously. I had a self-insert that was "the most human-like" partygoer, who was the daughter of Fillmore Feelgoode. The whole thing was magic-based; it was co-written by a few other people who were awesome and very talented, but I lost the domain and main site before I could do much more with it.

## Step 2?: Get Engaged to a Beautiful Woman

Around the same time I was making that, I joined a Discord server loosely connected to Night Mind… its members came from his Reddit and Patreon Discords… where I met my fiancée, who is the titular Beautiful Woman of this section. We started dating in 2018, and are still together today. I love her so much! She is a VTuber and my favorite one at that, and she recently started streaming again, so [check](https://www.twitch.tv/itismoto) [her](https://www.youtube.com/@itismoto) [out!](https://www.youtube.com/@MotoStreamDatabase) (three links there).

## Step 3: The KIN ARG

In 2022, I made an ARG for her birthday that ran through the year to our anniversary. Utilizing the stimulus checks we got that year, I paid a pair of voice actors to help me make a web series and ARG. I bought a domain and set up a website where I posted an ARG alongside [the web series I edited on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@KINtheARG) throughout the year; it now lives at [a subdomain](https://kinland.helltop.net) on this site.

She loved it! It also taught her some of my favorite simple puzzles and ciphers so she could follow the story. I made the final puzzle specifically for her to solve, and it's very difficult to solve from scratch.

## Conclusion: ARGs Rule

My fiancee and I both love ARGs and such. I may make more in the future if people are interested! Reply to me on [Wafrn](https://app.wafrn.net/blog/lucyinchat) to comment on this blog.
