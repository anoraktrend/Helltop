## Introduction

Halloween is my favorite holiday, it's such a nice time of year! The falling leaves and the cooler weather... and my favorite holiday for the whipped cream on top of the pumpkin pie! 

It's no secret my favorite TV series to watch within the month of October is [The Addams Family](https://www.justwatch.com/us/tv-show/the-addams-family), but what about my other favorite spooky media? 

## Music

- The Monster Mash: The Mash. The original.
- Spooky, Scary Skeletons: A Beloved internet classic, defined the season for my generation in a way
- Thriller: It's Thriller. 'nuff said. 
- Ghostbusters: Who you gonna call?

## Movies

- Hocus Pocus: Beware... a virgin has lit the Black Flame Candle, the Sanderson Sisters have returned. 
- Scooby Doo (2002): My moms introduced me to the Scooby Doo Live Action movies. They're very good october movies.
- The Nightmare Before Christmas: Who can forget the best movie of both seasons?
- Scary Godmother: Scary Godmother is a fantastic kids book and not for kids indy comics. The Movies are cheap old CGI and I can't imagine it any different.

## Games

- Doki Doki Literature Club: A Horror game mascurading as a dating sim...
- Slay the Princess: A romance game with the vinear of a horror game...
- Pony Island: A horror game disguised as a kids arcade game...
- Buddy Simulator 1984: A complex "AI" horror game which starts as a text adventure
